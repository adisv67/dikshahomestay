Things to do around Bhandardara

Explore the unspoiled beauty, ancient forts, and majestic waterfalls

Bhandardara Dam

Bhandardara is one of the few remaining unspoiled places where nature is in its most natural state, promising relaxation and authenticity.

  • Unique: Oldest dam (Built in 1910)
  • Points to see: Umbrella fall, Garden, Spill way
  • Activity: Boating, Swimming

Amruteshwar Temple

An intricately carved Shiva temple in Ratanwadi, over 1200 years old, built by the Shilahara dynasty in the 9th century CE.

  • Unique: Oldest Temple (1200 Yrs Old)
  • Points to see: TirthaKund
  • Activity: Meditate, Spiritual Prays

Randha Fall

The River Pravara descends from a height of 170 ft into a gorge. Best viewed during monsoons with amazing new viewpoints.

  • Unique: 170 ft height
  • Points to see: Ghorpada Devi temple, River Bridge
  • Activity: Swimming, Spiritual Prays

Sandhan Valley

Known as the Valley of Shadows, this spectacular canyon in the Sahyadri mountains offers one of the most challenging and enjoyable treks.

  • Unique: 200 ft deep
  • Points to see: Asia's 2nd Largest deep valley
  • Activity: Trekking, Rappelling

Fireflies

Just before the monsoons, tiny bioluminescence flies shine with a natural glow to attract mates. Absolutely harmless and marvelous.

  • Unique: Kajava Mahotsav
  • Points to see: Wild life
  • Activity: Star Gazing

Ratangad Fort

400 years old fort captured by Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj. Features a natural rock peak called 'Nedhe' or 'Eye of the Needle'.

  • Unique: Tryambak Darwaza
  • Points to see: Wild life, Eye of the Needle
  • Activity: Hiking, Trekking

Kalsubai

The highest peak in Maharashtra, lying within the Kalsubai Harishchandragad Wildlife Sanctuary. Visited by avid trekkers and devotees alike.

  • Unique: Mt. Everest of Maharashtra (1646 ft)
  • Points to see: Wild life, Top view
  • Activity: Hiking, Trekking

Reverse Falls

A cascading beauty that defies gravity. The strong force of the winds pushes the water upwards, creating a reverse effect.

  • Unique: Reverse water flow
  • Points to see: Wild life, KokanKda
  • Activity: Enjoying the rain

Ghatghar Project

Two associated gravity dams built using roller-compacted concrete with reversible Francis turbine-generators, the first use in India.

  • Unique: Gravity dams
  • Points to see: Wild life
  • Activity: Trekking, Swimming

Harishchandragad

A 6th-century ancient fort with references in Matsyapurana and Skandapurana. The various caves were probably carved out in the 11th century.

  • Unique: Kedareshwar Cave
  • Points to see: Kokan Kada, Taramati peak, Temple
  • Activity: Trekking, Hiking, Rappelling

Necklace Fall

A uniquely shaped fall visible only in the rainy season. The greenery makes it an attractive place for photoshoots and golden moments.

  • Unique: Necklace shape
  • Points to see: Wild life
  • Activity: Trekking, Hiking

Wind Mills

In the heart of Sahyadri, 300+ wind mills are installed with high capacity turbines. The Western Ghats is a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

  • Unique: High capacity turbines
  • Points to see: Wild life
  • Activity: Hiking
